Have you ever found yourself frustrated with how things used to work before technology took over? Think about the early days of computers when they were slow, bulky, and could only perform fundamental tasks. In those days, computers were so limited that even simple calculations could take several minutes! Let’s take a trip down memory lane. Back in the day, everything was done manually, and that often meant long queues, bulky paper files, and plenty of unnecessary stress. Picture this: you go to the bank just to withdraw or deposit money. You join a long line under the hot sun, holding your withdrawal slip and hoping the cashier doesn’t close for lunch before your turn comes. Transactions were written in huge ledgers, and one tiny mistake could keep you standing there for hours while the staff “balanced the books.” Or think about how students used to register for courses in universities. You had to travel all the way to campus, fill several forms by hand, and queue up again to submit them, only to be told later that your name wasn’t “on the list.” Imagine doing that for every semester! These situations were frustrating, time-consuming, and often costly. Now compare that to today, you can transfer money from Ilorin to Lagos in seconds using a mobile app. You can register for your courses, download learning materials, and even attend virtual classes from home. if airplanes had advanced as quickly as computers have, you could probably buy a small personal aircraft for the price of a bicycle and fly from Lagos to Abuja in under five minutes.
